# B1 summary: low-pass, Kalman and baseline speed loops on the target

MEASURED on the ESP32-S3, rule-based manager in every arm, US06, plan item B1
(`docs/2026-10-14-measurement-plan.md`, H4 and B1). Source files:
`docs/2026-10-14-lowpass-pil-{b0,lowpass,kalman}-s{100,200}.json`, collected in
`docs/2026-10-14-lowpass-pil-summary.json`. No run was repeated for this summary.

## Record check

- Twelve accepted runs: three arms x two blocks x (floor, severe). Each file
  holds exactly one floor and one severe run.
- Seeds: block 100 is floor seed 100 and severe seed 102; block 200 is floor 200
  and severe 202. The same seed is shared across arms within a block, but a
  floor and a severe run are different noise draws.
- Disturbed record kept: `docs/2026-10-14-lowpass-pil-b0-s200-disturbed.json`
  holds the baseline floor seed 200 run with 21 dropped frames (190.96 Wh/km),
  set aside under the fixed 16-frame rule and taken again (190.89 Wh/km, 7
  frames).
- Arm identity: Kalman runs report `speed_est_mode` 3; baseline and low-pass
  report 0, and `actor_steps` is 0 in all twelve. The low-pass and baseline
  images cannot be told apart by the diagnostic counters (the smoothing
  constant is a compile switch that is not reported); their identity rests on
  the flash lines in `.claude/night/round2.log` (`b1-lowpass ... -DSPEED_KALMAN=0
  -DSPEED_FILTER_ALPHA=0.1f`; `b1-b0 ... -DSPEED_FILTER_ALPHA=1.0f`) and the
  separate build directories of `.claude/night/round2_board.sh`.

## What own-floor normalisation hides

- Clean energy: the low-pass floor is 0.38% (block 100) and 0.53% (block 200)
  above the baseline floor; the Kalman floor is -0.01% and +0.30%.
- Clean tracking: the low-pass floor RMSE is about three times the baseline
  and Kalman values (0.107 against 0.023 to 0.045 m/s).
- Absolute severe energy: Kalman 190.78 and 191.00, low-pass 192.00 and 191.51,
  baseline 200.80 and 200.63 Wh/km.
- The Kalman floor tracking here (0.035 and 0.045 m/s) is four to five times
  its earlier single run (0.009 m/s, `docs/week12-speed-arms-b5.json`); the
  cause is not established.

## Decision under the registered rule

Rule (fixed before the run): if the low-pass recovers at least as much as the
Kalman estimator, the paper credits filtering in general, not the Kalman
structure.

Outcome: the low-pass recovered less than the Kalman estimator in both blocks
(by 0.47 and 0.07 Wh/km), so the condition is not met and the rule does not
transfer the credit to filtering in general. The shortfall is of the order of
run-to-run spread (the baseline's floor runs differ by 0.26 Wh/km between
blocks), so the paper states it descriptively: both filters removed nearly all
of the 10x loss; the low-pass did so with a higher clean energy and about three
times the clean tracking error. Two blocks give descriptive spread, not a
population superiority or equivalence claim.

The earlier 9.44 to 0.24 Wh/km result (offset 0, `docs/week12-speed-arms-*.json`)
now has two more blocks: baseline 9.65 and 9.73, Kalman -0.36 and -0.48 Wh/km.
